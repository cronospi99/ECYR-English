📖 How to play

  • ☀️ Collect suns by clicking them: they fall from the sky and your sunflowers produce them.
  • 🌻 Pick a card and click a garden tile to plant.
  • ❓ To plant you must answer an English question. Correct → the plant grows! Wrong → you see the explanation and can try another.
  • ✨ Evolve your plants! With no card selected, tap a planted plant and answer a question to level it up: LVL 2 (double shot, stronger) → LVL 3 MAX (triple shot, tougher, more suns).
  • 🎈 Watch out for flying zombies — they float over your plants, so keep your shooters firing!
  • 🔥 Streak: every 3 correct answers in a row earns bonus suns.
  • 🧟 Don't let the zombies reach your house. The flying books are your last defense (one per lane).
  • ⛏️ Use the shovel to remove a plant and free the tile.
  • ⭐ Earn up to 3 stars per stage based on your English accuracy.
  • 🏺 Vase Breaker: answer to break vases — plants, treasures or zombies hide inside!
  • 🥔 Spud Bowling: click a lane to roll a spud. Answer questions to earn more spuds!
  • 📚 Almanac: tap any plant or zombie card to see its full stats — health, damage, range and, for plants, what each evolution level costs and gives you.
