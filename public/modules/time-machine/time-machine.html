Reading tip · the two roads of a year

Do you know how to pronounce the years?
1983
→
19
83
ROAD 1 · SPLIT THE YEAR
You separate the year in two parts. First you say nineteen (19), and then eighty-three (83).
“nineteen eighty-three”
Divides el año en dos partes: 19 + 83. Es la forma natural.
ROAD 2 · WHOLE NUMBER
You read the whole number, without splitting it. More formal, and longer.
“one thousand nine hundred eighty-three”
Lees el número completo, sin dividirlo. Más formal.
