WHAT IS A MODAL VERB?

A modal is a helping verb that does not describe an action. It describes the speaker's attitude towards the action: how possible it is, how necessary it is, how polite you are being about it.

FACT She works on Sundays.
ATTITUDE She might work / must work / should work on Sundays.

Same action. Four different worlds. That is the whole job of a modal.
